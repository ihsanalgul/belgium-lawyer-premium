# Cabinet Juridique International

## Register

**brand** — The design IS the product. Visitor impression of institutional credibility is the deliverable.

## Purpose

Single-page landing site for an international law firm operating in Ankara and Brussels. Converts corporate and cross-border clients into consultation inquiries.

## Target Users

- C-suite executives and general counsel evaluating counsel for cross-border transactions
- International investors requiring EU regulatory and Belgian corporate law expertise
- Turkish enterprises expanding into European markets (and reverse)

**Context:** Deliberate, high-stakes decision-making. Users arrive via referral, search, or direct URL. They need immediate signals of pedigree, discretion, and geographic reach.

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative · Discreet · Enduring**

Physical-object words: mahogany conference table, embossed letterhead, Brussels limestone facade, quiet corridor.

## Anti-References

- SaaS startup landing (purple gradients, mesh backgrounds, floating orbs)
- Generic three-equal-card feature grids
- Bounce scroll indicators, marquee text, parallax hero
- Buzzword copy ("seamless", "world-class", "cutting-edge")
- Playful or whimsical motion

## Strategic Design Principles

1. Editorial luxury over tech spectacle — magazine whitespace, asymmetric composition
2. Trust through restraint — gold accent used sparingly, never neon
3. Bilingual institutional tone — EN primary, FR and NL for Belgian market
4. Motion as gravitas — slow reveals, no floating elements
5. Accessibility WCAG AA minimum — contrast, keyboard nav, reduced motion

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for all body text (≥4.5:1)
- Focus-visible on all interactive elements
- Skip link to main content
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected globally
- Touch targets ≥44px

## Success Metrics

- Contact form submissions
- WhatsApp click-through
- Time on page indicating content engagement
